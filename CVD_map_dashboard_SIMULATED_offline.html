    Hypotheses

      What drives the GeoXGBoost predictions

      Average size of each contribution (GeoShapley), in deaths per 100 patients. “Location” is the part explained by where the area is, beyond the area characteristics in the model.

      How the numbers are calculated

      • Per 100 patients divides each count by the number of diabetes patients living in the area.
      • Smoothed applies Empirical Bayes smoothing, which pulls rates from small areas toward the overall average so that a handful of cases cannot produce an extreme value.
      • Rates are shown only for areas with at least diabetes patients. Counts from 1 to 4 are shown as “fewer than 5” to protect confidentiality.
      • Clusters use the Empirical Bayes–adjusted local Moran’s I (queen contiguity, permutations) on areas. Global Moran’s I = (p = ). Outlined areas have unadjusted p < 0.05; none remain significant after false discovery rate correction, so treat them as leads to investigate.
